Use ← ↑ → ↓ to move the selected stamp by 1 mm. Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl/Cmd+Y to redo.

How to use Stamp Layout Studio

  1. Choose A4, A6 Postcard, or enter a Custom Size.
  2. Create a saved stamp preset by choosing its shape and dimensions in millimeters.
  3. Click Add to canvas beside a preset.
  4. Drag, resize, or rotate stamps freely on the canvas. Touch/pointer input is supported on mobile.
  5. Use the Selected Stamp panel for exact X, Y, dimensions, and rotation.
  6. Use multiple canvases to prepare several layouts. Each canvas becomes one PDF page when you export.
  7. Choose the PDF paper size (A4 by default). Each canvas is placed at its real millimeter size and centered on its PDF page. The canvas border and 50 mm calibration line can be shown or hidden.

Printing: choose 100% / Actual Size in your printer dialog. Turn off “Fit to page” or automatic scaling.
