How to use Stamp Layout Studio

  1. Choose A4, A6 Postcard, or enter a Custom Size.
  2. Create a stamp preset by choosing its shape and dimensions in millimeters.
  3. Click Add to canvas beside a preset.
  4. Drag, resize, or rotate stamps freely on the canvas.
  5. Use the Selected Stamp panel for exact X, Y, width, height, and rotation values.
  6. Click Download PDF to create a print-ready PDF at the selected paper size.

Printing: choose 100% / Actual Size in your printer dialog. Turn off “Fit to page” or automatic scaling.
